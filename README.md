### 👨‍💼 About Me
 
I'm a Bangladeshi researcher, engineer, and public sector professional with multidisciplinary expertise spanning **Computer Science, Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Information Systems, and Public Financial Management**.
 
I currently serve as a **Junior Consultant (Executive)** at the Monitoring Cell of the Finance Division, Ministry of Finance, Government of Bangladesh — contributing to governance, monitoring, and financial oversight of State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) and Autonomous Bodies (ABs) under the **Strengthening Public Financial Management Program to Enable Service Delivery (SPFMS)**.
 
With 15+ years of experience across government service, engineering, academia, IT, renewable energy, and research, I'm passionate about applying emerging technologies to improve transparency, accountability, and efficiency in public institutions.
